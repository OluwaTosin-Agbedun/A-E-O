The Athena Post-Election Audit

A permanent public record of how Nigeria's elections perform.

APEA reconstructs the evidence chain from accreditation through the polling-unit result, collation and final declaration — then tests whether the process worked, where it failed, how serious the failures were, whether they could approach the declared outcome, and whether integrity is improving over time.

  • Every audited election, one standard assessment
  • Every non-compliant polling unit, published
  • Process integrity and outcome materiality, reported separately
  • Comparable across states and cycles
APEA is the public audit product. APAF is the methodology and scoring framework behind it.

How APEA reads an election

One structure, applied every time

Three integrity dimensions feed the overall assessment. Outcome materiality is reported separately, so a large winning margin never conceals a poorly run process.

01

Technology & Evidence

BVAS accreditation baseline, IReV transmission, and the availability and legibility of the statutory records.

02

Polling-Unit Compliance

Statutory over-voting under s.51(2), arithmetic integrity within the material band, ballot accounting and excluded records.

03

Collation Integrity

EC8A→EC8B alterations, transfer of over-voting, unexplained inflation, and EC8B→EC8C→EC8D reconciliation.
